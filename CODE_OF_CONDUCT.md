# Code of Conduct

Sulai welcomes participation across backgrounds, identities, abilities, and levels
of experience. Everyone, including maintainers, is expected to help keep project
spaces safe for constructive technical work.

## Expected behavior

- Address ideas and evidence rather than attacking people. Disagreement about a
  design or decision is welcome; insults and intimidation are not.
- Give actionable feedback, respect others' time, and make room for questions.
- Respect privacy, personal boundaries, and requests to stop unwanted contact.
- Accept moderation decisions while using the reporting process to raise concerns.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, publication of private
information without consent, sustained disruption, and retaliation against people
who report concerns are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, Discussions,
and other project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone is representing
Sulai in a public setting. It does not prohibit good-faith technical criticism.

## Reporting and response

Send reports privately to
[suriagajohncyrus@gmail.com](mailto:suriagajohncyrus@gmail.com), monitored by the
lead maintainer, [@jcsuriaga](https://github.com/jcsuriaga). Include relevant links,
what happened, and any immediate safety concerns. Do not post a report containing
personal information in a public issue.

The maintainer will assess the report, seek additional context when needed, and
share information only as needed to respond. Absolute confidentiality cannot be
guaranteed. Depending on severity and recurrence, responses may include a warning,
content removal, or temporary or permanent participation restrictions. Reasons
should be explained to those affected when safe to do so.

You may request reconsideration by replying with additional context. The project
currently has one maintainer and no independent internal appeals body. GitHub's
own abuse-reporting tools remain available for conduct on its platform, including
concerns involving a maintainer.
